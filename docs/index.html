AI Deviation Grill — two agents stress-test a deviation

One AI plays the investigation team. The other interrogates it, one question at a time.

The case (synthetic): a clean-in-place log said the cycle finished clean at 14:00; the equipment audit trail said a pressure alarm forced a restart to 15:47. A valid microbial hit was written off as a "sampling error," and ~50,000 units were released on that basis. Watch the grill drive the conclusion to a latent validation gap — not operator error.
  • 34/34 planted gaps surfaced across 7 seeded test cases (100% recall on defect-bearing cases)
  • Refused "operator error" closures; rejected retraining-only CAPAs
  • On a genuinely clean record it over-reached — a real, documented limitation

The skill, the four-agent evaluation harness, the scores, and the transcripts are all open. Interrogation discipline adapted from Matt Pocock's grill-me (credited in the repo).

All examples use synthetic data from a fictional, deliberately-unnamed pharmaceutical company, generated for research and educational purposes. This is a research experiment and a multi-agent demo — not a validated GxP system, and not advice for real investigations. The video is fully synthetic (AI avatars + voices).

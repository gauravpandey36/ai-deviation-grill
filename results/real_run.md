## Interpretation & honest caveats

**Read the 100% flags carefully — they are partly an input artifact, not proof of 13 broken investigations.**

- The audited inputs were the **deviation records alone**. Each record references its CAPA by ID
  (e.g. `CAPA-GC-2025-031`) but does **not embed the CAPA body**. So when the grill flags
  "CAPA not linked to root cause" (13/13) and "missing/weak effectiveness check" (13/13), it is
  correctly reporting that *it could not verify linkage or an effectiveness check from the record
  it was given* — the CAPA content lives in a separate linked record that was not provided. This
  is a **data-scoping limitation of the audit input**, not evidence that all 13 CAPAs were
  deficient. A fair production run would feed the grill the deviation **and** its linked CAPA /
  change-control records.
- Similarly "scope gap" (13/13) and "unresolved conflict" (12/13) are inflated by single-pass
  audit mode: with no investigation team to answer back, the grill cannot confirm that scope was
  assessed elsewhere, so it flags the absence of that evidence in the record.
- The **less artifact-prone signals** are the informative ones:
  - **Human-error-only root cause flagged: 2/13 (15%)**
  - **Retraining-only CAPA flagged: 2/13 (15%)**
  - Substantive root-cause-vs-evidence catches (e.g. DEV-GC-2025-018: the documented dark-brown,
    rapidly-settling particle morphology does not match the silicone-oil protein-aggregation root
